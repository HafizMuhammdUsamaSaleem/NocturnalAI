# Kestrel Industries: travel documents rules (fictional)

- **Alerts:** 90, 30 and 7 days before a document expires, tell the holder and their PA (if they have one). For company documents, tell the listed contact and admin@kestrel.example.
- **Each threshold alert is sent once.** When a document expires, tell the holder and admin that day, then weekly until it's updated.
- **Weekly digest:** Mondays at 09:00 to admin@kestrel.example. It covers everything expiring in the next 90 days, everything expired, and any data problems.
- **Travel check:**
  - A passport must be valid for **at least 6 months after the return date**.
  - Our travellers hold Pakistani passports and need a visa (or e-visa) for: Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, France, Italy (Schengen visa); the United Kingdom; the United States; China; the United Arab Emirates; Turkey.
  - Flag any trip that fails either check, as early as possible.
- **Privacy:** never send a full document number by chat or email; show the last 3 characters only ("passport ending 203"). Only the admin office may read the full data.

All dates are written the Pakistani way (day first) unless they are ISO (YYYY-MM-DD).
