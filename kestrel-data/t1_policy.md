# Kestrel Industries: approval policy (fictional)

- **Working hours:** Monday to Saturday, 09:00–18:00 PKT. **Saturday 10 October 2026: factory closed** (annual maintenance).
- **Reminder:** when a request has waited **4 working hours** at its current stage, remind the approver. Repeat every 4 working hours while it waits.
- **Escalation:** when it has waited **9 working hours** (one working day), also tell the approver's manager.
- **Director co-approval:** a request of **PKR 500,000 and above** needs the Director Finance's approval *after* the manager's, unless the Director Finance is already the approver. Convert other currencies at these rates (14 Oct 2026): 1 USD = PKR 280.40, 1 EUR = PKR 305.60.
- **Evening summary:** at 18:00 on working days, one message to the Director Finance:
  - what was approved and rejected today
  - what is still pending, oldest first: who it's waiting on, and for how long
- **No nagging:** nobody gets more than one reminder per request in any 4 working hours.
- Approvers will not log in to anything. They decide from their phone.

`stage` in the requests file is the approval the request is waiting for: `manager` (the approver) or `director` (the Director Finance).
