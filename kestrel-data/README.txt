Kestrel Industries: assessment data pack
=========================================

Everything here is fictional: the company, people, phone numbers, emails, documents and figures.
Phone numbers are deliberately invalid and emails use the reserved .example domain.
Send every test message to yourself.

Scenario clock: Thursday 15 October 2026, 08:30 PKT (Asia/Karachi, UTC+5).
Make 'now' a setting, so your automation can be replayed at the scenario time.
